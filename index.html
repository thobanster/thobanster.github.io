Imran Thobani

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in Ophthalmology at Stanford University. I am a member of the NeuroAILab (advised by Dan Yamins) as well as a member of the Enigma project (advised by Andreas Tolias).

My research focuses on developing large-scale predictive models of the brain to gain scientific insights and for downstream biomedical applications. I completed my PhD at Stanford in 2025, where my research combined philosophy of neuroscience and computational neuroscience.
